Benchmark1:
	arrays of size: 10,000 ints
	trials: 1000
	How fast can can the CPU do 10 million additions (10,000*1000)?


	How fast should the CPU do 10M additions?
		assuming 1 adds per clock cycle
			4.5e9 adds per second
			10e6/4.5e9 = 0.002222 seconds ~ 22.22ms
		assuming 2 adds per clock cycle
			2*4.5e9 adds per second
			10e6/9.0e9 = 0.0111 seconds ~ 11.11ms
